|------|----------|--------------|---------------------|------------|
| script_check | Minor | Diagnostics lack real line/column info | Expected: line-level errors per the tool's own description; Actual: every diagnostic is a generic {line:0, "call editor_get_console"} stub, on both 6.4/6.5 and 6.6's dedicated check | Use editor_get_console or lsp_diagnostics for precise error location (S6.6). |
| resource_delete / asset_list | Low | Index staleness after deleting a resource | Expected: immediate deindex plus fresh reads after delete; Actual: asset_list still listed a deleted .tres and resource_load kept serving an in-memory cached copy until a 2nd delete plus a full editor_refresh | Call resource_delete a second time then editor_refresh(mode=full) to fully clear the leftover (S22 C6, c6_ts.tres). |
